V2.4 UPGRADE: Deterministic preload via spacer tube

Architectural shift from V2.3: Preload no longer depends on hand-tightening the top collar to a specific position. Instead, a precision spacer tube (29.40mm long) on the shaft between the two bearing inner races forces the wave spring into exactly 0.5mm of compression - delivering ~30N preload automatically and repeatably. This is how industrial CNC spindles and Lonati-class knitting heads work.

Top collar role changed: now retention only (light clamp), not preload setting. Result: zero assembly variability, zero drift over time.

V2.4 Assembly Procedure

Key difference from V2.3: No more "push shaft 1mm and tighten collar" step. The spacer tube does the compression automatically. Assembly is simpler and more repeatable.

Block Gap Verification (CRITICAL)

Block gap MUST be 11mm

The spacer tube length (29.40mm) was calculated based on:

  • 6mm aluminum mounting plate
  • 5mm assembly clearance (for fasteners and access)
  • Total: 11mm gap between top of bottom block and bottom of top block

If your actual block gap differs from 11mm, recalculate the spacer length:

Spacer length = (Block gap) + 18.4mm

Examples: gap = 10mm → spacer 28.4mm. gap = 12mm → spacer 30.4mm.

Step-by-step Assembly

  1. Print all 3 STLs from V2.4 macro (top block, bottom block, spacer tube)
  2. Apply Loctite 603 to bearing pockets, install:
    • 51101 thrust bearing in bottom block lower pocket
    • 6001-2RS radial bearing in bottom block upper pocket
    • 6001-2RS radial bearing in top block
  3. Cure Loctite 24 hours at room temperature
  4. In bottom block, stack: lower 2x AS1226 washers → wave spring → spacer ring (no Loctite on spring/spacer/washers)
  5. Mount bottom block to aluminum plate using M5 flathead screws
  6. Mount top block to a separate plate using M5 flathead screws
  7. Verify 11mm gap between blocks (use spacer tube as gauge - it should JUST barely touch both bearing inner races when unloaded)
  8. Install bottom shaft collar at Z = -2mm (clamped tightly)
  9. Insert shaft through bottom block
  10. Slide spacer tube onto shaft (sits on bottom radial bearing inner race)
  11. Lower top block onto shaft (top bearing slides over shaft, contacts top of spacer tube)
  12. Bolt top plate to frame (this defines block gap)
  13. Install top shaft collar - LIGHTLY clamp (retention only, no compression force)

Test Protocol (Verify Preload Works)

  1. Rotation test: Spin shaft by hand. Should feel smooth with slight resistance.
  2. Axial play test: Try to lift/push shaft. Should be ZERO movement.
  3. Click test: Rotate slowly listening for clicks. Should be silent.
  4. Drag test: Spin shaft, time to coastdown. Should rotate freely 5+ seconds.
  5. Runout test: Use dial indicator. <0.2mm TIR at shaft end.
  6. Heat test: Run 30 RPM for 10 minutes. Should remain cool to touch.

Fine-Tuning (if any test fails)

SymptomCauseFix
Click/rattle on rotationSpacer tube too LONG (insufficient preload)Shorten spacer 0.1-0.2mm OR remove a shim from the wave spring stack
Heavy drag, stiff rotationSpacer tube too SHORT (excessive preload)Add 0.1mm shim above the spacer ring
Heat buildupExcessive preloadSame as above - reduce preload
Axial play (~0.1-0.5mm)Spacer too long OR spring damagedShorten spacer; inspect spring for plastic deformation
