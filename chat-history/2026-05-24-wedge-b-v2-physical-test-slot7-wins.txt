MECHANICAL IMPLICATIONS OF DEEPER SLOT + GROOVE
================================================================

V3.0 (old)                          V3.1 (new)
---------                           ----------
SLOT_DEPTH      = 3.00 mm           SLOT_DEPTH      = 4.70 mm
GROOVE_DEPTH    = 1.30 mm           GROOVE_DEPTH    = 3.10 mm
Slot bottom radius = 54.15 mm       Slot bottom radius = 52.45 mm
Wall remaining   = 10.15 mm         Wall remaining   = 8.45 mm
Spring inner     = 55.85 mm         Spring inner     = 54.05 mm
Needle outer     = 55.95 mm         Needle outer     = 54.25 mm
Spring preload   = +0.10 mm         Spring preload   = +0.20 mm
Spring protrusion = +1.49 mm (past OD)  Spring protrusion = -0.31 mm (RECESSED)

Key wins of V3.1:
 1. Better needle retention (deeper slot grip + 2x preload margin)
 2. Spring no longer protrudes past OD -- no cam ring interference risk
 3. Still 8.45 mm of wall material -- plenty of structural margin

The recessed spring is mechanically SUPERIOR to the V3.0 protruding
spring because:
 - Won't snag on yarn carriers or feeders during rotation
 - Won't foul cam ring inner surface (cam ID 115 - cylinder OD 114.3
   = 0.35 mm/side clearance; V3.0 spring ate ~1.5 mm of that budget)
 - Hidden inside the cylinder profile -- cleaner geometry
