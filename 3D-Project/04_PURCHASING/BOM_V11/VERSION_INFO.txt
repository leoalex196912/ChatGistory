KEY DECISIONS BY ITERATION:

V10 → V11 Changes:
  • Servo: SG90 (4-pack) → MG90S (8-pack metal gear)
    Cost +$147, but eliminates 50-100 hour failure window
  • Springs: Updated to actual sources (McMaster 3741N653, FlyDesigns)
  • Washers: M20 stainless from Amazon (easier to source than custom)
  • Wood base: 420×400 → 500×400mm (for 6-feeder layout)
  • Base material: Stick with wood for prototype (upgrade to aluminum later)

ENGINEERING DECISIONS LOCKED:

Cam Ring V6.5 FINAL:
  • Smoothstep cubic easing (jerk-free transitions)
  • 8mm lift (conservative, tunable up via Z_OFFSET)
  • 6.0mm slot (full clearance outside knit zone)
  • 5.2mm slot with smoothstep-blended lip in knit zone
  • Mandatory finishing: sand 400→1000 grit + PTFE lubricant
  
Servo Choice:
  • MG90S metal-gear (100k+ cycles, 800+ hours)
  • vs SG90 plastic (10-20k cycles, 50-100 hours failure)
  • 8× total: 6 for feeders + 2 spares

Spring Sources:
  • Cylinder Springs: FlyDesigns (authentic, from machine supplier)
  • Wave Springs: McMaster 3741N653 (equivalent to Smalley CM25-L1)
  • Both confirmed by engineering review

BASE DECISION:

  • 500×400mm locked (supports 6-feeder layout)
