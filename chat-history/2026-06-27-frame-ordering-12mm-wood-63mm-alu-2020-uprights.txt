KEY DESIGN DECISIONS LOCKED THIS SESSION
================================================================

1. Aluminum plate is 6.3 mm (1/4"), 6061-T6, NOT 4.7 mm
   - Stiffness > weight savings here
   - Flatness retention critical for master datum

2. Wood is 12 mm Baltic Birch ply, NOT 18 mm hardwood
   - Supplier availability dictated
   - Master datum at Z=230 preserved by lengthening uprights

3. 2020 uprights are 200 mm long (cut from 500 mm V-slot sticks)
   - Practical cut length absorbs the 0.3 mm CAD-vs-practical delta
   - V-slot fully compatible with T-nuts in user's inventory

4. Frame deliverables = STEP + DXF + PDF per part
   - STEP is the unambiguous thickness reference
   - DXF is the 2D cut path for CNC software
   - PDF is human-readable drawing

5. NEVER 3D-print the wood/aluminum structural parts
   - Wood/aluminum is cheaper, stiffer, more dimensionally stable
   - 3D printing reserved for cassette + tooling + brackets
